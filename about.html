About Ecotrace

Bridging the gap between environmental conservation and advanced technology.

Our Mission

Ecotrace was born from a simple realization: while millions of trees are planted every year, tracking their growth and survival at scale remains a massive challenge. Traditional manual monitoring is slow, expensive, and often inaccurate.

We leverage AI-powered satellite imagery to provide NGOs, governments, and communities with real-time, verifiable data on their reforestation efforts. Our platform ensures transparency and helps maximize the environmental impact of every sapling planted.

Global Reach

Monitoring projects across 5 continents.

AI Driven

Using neural networks for precise growth analysis.

Why Plantation Tracking Matters

Climate Impact

Accurate tracking allows us to calculate precise carbon sequestration metrics, vital for climate goals.

Accountability

Verifiable data builds trust with donors and stakeholders, ensuring funds are used effectively.

Survival Rates

Monitoring helps identify areas where trees are struggling, allowing for timely intervention.
